What is Cyberbullying?

Cyberbullying is when someone uses the internet, games, or social media to be mean, tease, or hurt someone. It can happen in messages, posts, comments, or online games.

Examples of Cyberbullying

  • Mean messages or emails
  • Spreading rumors online
  • Posting embarrassing photos or videos without permission
  • Excluding someone from online games or chats

How to Handle Cyberbullying

  • Don’t respond to the bully
  • Save evidence like screenshots of messages
  • Tell an adult you trust – parent, teacher, or guardian
  • Block or report the person on the platform
  • Remember: It’s not your fault – bullies are responsible for their actions

Tips to Stay Safe Online

  • Be kind to others online, just like in real life
  • Keep your accounts private and only accept friend requests from people you know
  • Think before you post – once it’s online, it can be hard to remove
  • Balance your time online – play, learn, and rest your eyes

Fun Example

Imagine someone keeps sending you mean messages in a game. ❌ Don’t reply! Instead, block them and tell a parent. It’s like walking away from someone teasing you in real life.
