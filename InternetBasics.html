Let's Learn Internet Basics

What is the Internet?

The internet is like a giant library, playground, and TV all combined. You can use it to:

  • Watch videos on YouTube.
  • Play games with friends online.
  • Do your schoolwork and research projects.
  • Chat or video call with family and friends.

Example: Just like you can borrow books from a library, you can “borrow” information from websites. But unlike a library, anyone can put anything online, so you have to be careful about what you read or click.

Websites and Apps

Websites are like houses on the internet. Each website has different rooms (pages) and things to do. Apps are tools or games you can download on your phone, tablet, or computer. Some websites and apps are free, and some need payment or subscriptions.

Example: A game like Minecraft is an app, while a site like National Geographic Kids is a website where you can read articles and watch videos.

Public vs Private

Not everything on the internet is meant for everyone to see.

  • Public: Anyone can see it, like YouTube comments or TikTok videos if your account isn’t private.
  • Private: Only people you choose can see it, like your school email or messages to your friends.

Tip: Never share your full name, address, or school name on public platforms.

Fun Internet Rules for Kids

  • Ask before you click: If a link or pop-up looks weird, ask an adult.
  • Keep your secrets safe: Passwords are private.
  • Be kind online: Treat people online the same way you’d treat friends in real life.
  • Balance your time: Don’t spend all day online—play outside, read, and rest your eyes.

Cool Fact

Did you know there are over 1.5 billion websites on the internet? That’s a LOT of information! But only some of it is safe and true, so being smart online is super important.

Mini Challenge: Find 3 websites that teach something new to you today. Show your parents and explain what you learned. Remember to check if the website is safe (look for a lock icon in the URL bar).
