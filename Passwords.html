How to Manage Passwords & Ensure Account Safety?

A password is like a secret key that protects your online accounts. Only you (and your parents) should know it. Think of it as your treasure chest key for your digital world!

How to make a strong password:

  • Use a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.
  • Make it long (at least 8 characters).
  • Avoid using your name, birthday, or “12345”.
  • Use a fun phrase you can remember. Example: “I love my cat Max!” → ILmcM!2025

Keep your accounts safe:

  • Never share your passwords with friends or strangers.
  • Use different passwords for different accounts.
  • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) for extra security.
  • Log out on shared devices.
  • Ask a parent before downloading new apps or games.

Mini Challenge: Create a strong password using your favorite hobby or animal and test it safely with a parent’s help!
