What is Privacy?

Privacy means keeping certain things about yourself secret so only people you trust can see them. On the internet, some information should stay private, like your full name, address, phone number, school name, or passwords.

Why Privacy Matters

  • Keeps you safe from strangers who might try to trick you.
  • Protects your accounts and personal stuff from being stolen.
  • Helps you control who sees your pictures, videos, and posts.

Tips to Protect Your Privacy

  • Keep passwords secret – don’t share them, even with friends.
  • Check privacy settings on apps and games to control who can see your posts.
  • Think before posting – once it’s online, it’s hard to remove.
  • Ask an adult if a website or app asks for personal info.

Fun Example

Imagine a stranger sends you a message saying: "Send me a photo of your house and I’ll give you free coins for your game." Would you do it? ❌ Of course not! This is why privacy is super important.
