What is Safe Gaming?

Safe gaming means playing video games in a way that keeps you happy, healthy, and protected online. Games are fun, but it’s important to stay safe while playing.

Why Safe Gaming Matters

  • Keeps your personal info and accounts secure.
  • Helps you avoid mean players or online scams.
  • Makes sure you don’t spend too much time and stay healthy.

Tips for Safe Gaming

  • Never share your password or account info with anyone.
  • Play with friends or family you know – avoid strangers.
  • Check game ratings to make sure they’re okay for your age.
  • Take breaks – rest your eyes and stretch.
  • Ask an adult if a game asks for money or personal info.

Fun Example

Imagine a game says: "Click here to get free coins!" and asks for your password. ❌ Don’t click! This is a trick to steal your account.
