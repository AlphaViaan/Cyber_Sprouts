What are Scams?

A scam is when someone tries to trick you into giving away your money, personal details, or account information. Online scammers often pretend to be friendly, but they really want to steal something from you.

Examples of Scams

  • Fake emails saying "You won a free iPhone!" and asking you to click a link.
  • Messages from someone pretending to be your favorite game asking for your password.
  • Strangers offering money if you give them your bank details.

Common Online Threats

  • Phishing: Fake emails or websites that try to steal your info.
  • Viruses & Malware: Bad software that can damage your computer or steal data.
  • Fake Profiles: People pretending to be someone else to trick you.

How to Stay Safe

  • Don't click suspicious links – ask an adult first.
  • Never share personal info like your password, address, or school name.
  • Check who sent the message – real companies won’t ask for your password.
  • Use antivirus software and keep it updated.

Fun Example

Imagine someone knocks on your door and says: "Hi, I’m from your bank. Please give me your piggy bank key so I can check it." Would you trust them? ❌ No! That’s the same way you should treat strange messages online.
