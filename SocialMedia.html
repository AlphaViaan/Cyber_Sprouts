What is Social Media?

Social media is any website or app where you can share messages, photos, and videos and talk to friends. Examples: TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat, YouTube, or school chat apps.

Why Social Media Can Be Fun

  • Stay in touch with friends and family.
  • Share your artwork, videos, or cool discoveries.
  • Learn new things from safe pages or channels.

How to Stay Safe on Social Media

  • Think before you post – once it’s online, it can be seen forever.
  • Don’t share personal info like your address, school, or full name.
  • Only friend people you know and trust.
  • Be kind online – treat others the way you want to be treated.
  • Report anything weird or mean to a parent or teacher.

Fun Example

Imagine a friend posts a funny video of you without asking. How would you feel? 😟 Always ask before sharing someone else’s picture or video, and never post mean comments.

👉 Mini Challenge: Check your favorite social media app and make sure only trusted friends can see your posts. Leave one kind comment for a friend today!
